Nowruz is celebrated every year on March 20–21,  making the beginning of spring and the spring equinox.

The celebrations begin four weeks before Nowruz with four traditional Wednesdays (Charshanbas). These are celebrated one per week and symbolize the awakening of nature:
	1.	Water Wednesday – purification and the source of life
	2.	Fire Wednesday – warmth, energy, and renewal
	3.	Wind Wednesday – movement and change
	4.	Earth Wednesday – awakening of the soil and fertility

After these four Wednesdays, Nowruz arrives and spring officially begins 🌸
