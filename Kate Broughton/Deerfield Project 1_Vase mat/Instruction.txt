So what do  I  suggest?  1)  do  it  without  lining  2)  stitch  it  on a tearaway  stabilizer  and  remove  it  from the back side.
Regarding the stabilizer. Rule #1 in embroidery: use a cut-away stabilizer! If it is not suitable for your project, consider another stabilizer. The stabilizer with an invisible mesh gives the fabric a little more flexibility (  but  I  do  not  try  it,  I will  do  it  a  bit  later then  I  will  prepare  designs  for the shop). But keep in mind, the tear-away stabilizer is very sensitive to hot ironing and squeezing under  high temperature . So the linen should be well pre-washed for it, if  possible.  My opinion and my choice would be to sew this pocket design on a tear-away stabilizer.  

 You  have 2  options on how  to  finish the edge. 1)  You  can  cut fabric   in  the  hoop and  the stabilizer will  be  cut or tear-away after  embroidery  is  finished. This  way  gives  you  a  bit  cleaner looking   edge  2)  Cut  fabric  and  stabilizer after   embroidery is completed.  But  you  need  to  do  it  more  carefully,  because it's  2  layers  for  cut and it  much  easier  to  cut  edges  threat .

Another  thing.  You  can   hoop  fabric,  but    you  can  also  float  it.  Linen  isn't  cheaper,  you  will  save  a  lot  if you float.
  
Instruction: - First  line  in the design  shows  design  placement.  Stitch  it  directly  on the stabilizer.  
- Float fabric  in  a  bit  more  than   design  size,  secure  it ,  if  it is necessary  and  run the next  color.   This  line  will  tack  down  fabric  and  stabilizer. 
- Optional. If you  want  to  cut an edge in  the  hoop,  do it   now!
- Continue embroidery  until the end. 
- Unhoop everything  and cut the stabilizer  or  stabilizer  and  fabric  around  the  edge  and  press  it!
