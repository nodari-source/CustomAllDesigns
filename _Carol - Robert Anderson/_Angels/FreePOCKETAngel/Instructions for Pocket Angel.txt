GENERAL STITCHING INSTRUCTIONS
We caution against changing the size of these designs. Lace designs require precise stitch placement and lengths, and changing the size could destroy the integrity and/or appearance of the lace.


STABILIZER
We recommend two layers of heavy-weight water-soluble stabilizer, such as Badgemaster. This is free-standing lace, stitched on the stabilizer only, no fabric required.

THREAD
Please note: Thread should be 2-ply.
This design is best stitched with 50- or 60-wt. cotton or 40-wt. rayon or poly. Use a size 70/11 embroidery needle.

TENSIONS
Experimentation is important. Bobbin tension should be loose enough to create balanced tension. Otherwise, the bobbin thread may pull away from the top thread. The top thread should lie directly on top of the bobbin thread, with neither thread pulling toward the inside of curves, or looking loose or loopy.


REMOVING STABILIZER
Clip (don't tear) away the excess stabilizer. Place the design in a container of water and let soak for at least an hour, then rinse. Soak again in fresh water. Do this at least once more, after which soaking overnight is the best way to ensure that all stabilizer is removed.

After soaking, lay flat to dry. When dry, steam-press lightly.
