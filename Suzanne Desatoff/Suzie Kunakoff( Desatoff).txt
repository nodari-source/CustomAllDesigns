Plaine Candy  $30 

 2 cups sugar,
1 tablespoon flour,
¼ teaspoon salt,
2 tablespoons light corn syrup,
1 cup milk,
Mix together and  then cook to a Soft Ball on a candy thermometer, or until a few drops will form a soft ball in cold water.

Remove  from  Fire  and  add:
½ cup of chopped dates.

Return to fire  and simmer for 10 minutes.
Remove  from  fire and  add:

 2 tablespoons butter,
1 teaspoon vanilla,
 2 cups walnuts.

Beat until thickens and pour out on damp cloth and form a roll. Let dry and slice.
