The Wardrobe of Queen Maud of Norway
Queen Maud of Norway’s Evening Dress, by Laferrière, c. 1909, at the National Museum of Art, Architecture and Design.

Full view of the c. 1907 Laferrière gala gown from the wardrobe of queen Maud. Illuding a satin wrap gown over a metallic blouse, the construction is heavier and more boned than one might expect. The back offers a nice V-neckline, almost pinafore dress style, and there's also a long train. 
The dress is today in the care of The National Museum in Oslo (OK-1962-0013).

The ice green dress queen Maud wears in the aforementioned portrait and photographs. The dress was made by Laferrière in Paris between 1907 and 1909, and features a pinafore like construction of silk satin, embroidery and sequined golden lace. The dress is today in the care of The Narional Museum in Oslo (OK-1962-0013).
